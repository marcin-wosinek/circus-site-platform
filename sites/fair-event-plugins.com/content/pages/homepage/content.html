Ticketing software that only costs you something when you're making something.

How the pricing works

Fair Events is free to install. Fair Payments Connector is funded by a simple integration fee on ticket sales.

The integration fee is waived through 31 December 2026. From 1 January 2027, it is a flat 2% of ticket sales, collected automatically through the payment flow. If you sell nothing, there is no integration fee.

The 2% integration fee has no monthly cap. Mollie's payment-processing fees are separate and are charged according to your Mollie agreement.

You pay a clear, proportional fee only when you make ticket sales.

Your site, your data

No account on someone else's system. No audience data leaving your server. You own your attendees, your events, and your sales history.

Plugins

Fair Events

The core plugin. Adds a custom Event post type to WordPress with start times, end times, and all-day options. Comes with a calendar block and an events list block — no shortcodes, no page builders, just native Gutenberg. Everything your event page needs, nothing you don't.

Install fair-events →

Fair Payments Connector

The money layer. Handles online payments via Mollie — including webhooks, test/live modes, and automatic status updates for stuck transactions. Stores every sale as an itemised transaction, with a built-in ledger for budgets and bank reconciliation. Its integration fee is waived through 31 December 2026; from 1 January 2027 it is a flat, uncapped 2% of ticket sales. Mollie processing fees are separate.

Install fair-payments-connector →

Fair Timetable

A Gutenberg block system for multi-track schedules. Build a visual programme with columns (stages, rooms, tracks) and time slots that position themselves automatically based on start time. Responsive out of the box — time ranges collapse gracefully on mobile. Perfect for festivals, conferences, and workshops with parallel sessions.
