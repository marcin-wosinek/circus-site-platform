The sessions

Deep Relaxation Thai Yoga Massage

A full-body session genuinely adapted to you

Performed on a comfortable floor mat, Thai Yoga Massage is a holistic form of bodywork rooted in traditional Thai medicine, combining pressure and rhythmic compression with assisted movement, stretching and touch. Each session develops around you — how you are feeling that day, what you tell me and how your body responds. The work can range from subtle to intense, while respecting your comfort and limits. For me, Thai Yoga Massage is also an energetic meeting between practitioner and receiver, based on attention, listening and touch.

Relaxing face, head & neck massage

Focused touch, slower rhythms

This massage focuses on the face, scalp, neck and shoulders, with an emphasis on slower rhythms and attentive, responsive touch. Unlike Kobido, there is no structured protocol to follow. This gives me more freedom to adapt the pace, focus and quality of touch to the person receiving it. The focus is simple: a quiet, relaxing experience centred around touch.

Kobido

Japanese facial massage as bodywork

Kobido is a Japanese approach to facial massage characterised by a structured sequence of techniques and changing rhythms. Some sequences are quick and dynamic; others are slower and quieter. Although Kobido is often associated with beauty and appearance, this isn’t the main focus of my practice. I am more interested in Kobido as bodywork for the face — an opportunity to experience the face through touch and sensation, rather than only through how it looks.

OUR APPROACH TO BODYWORK

My background in dance, movement and somatic practice influences the way I work with touch — particularly my attention to weight, support, movement and stillness.

Technique matters, but so do attention, communication, boundaries and the quality of the encounter between practitioner and receiver.

I work with attention to the person receiving the massage and respect for their comfort and boundaries.
