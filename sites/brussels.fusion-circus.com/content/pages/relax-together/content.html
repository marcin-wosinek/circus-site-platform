A different way to spend time together

Looking for something beyond the usual dinner, drinks or sitting on the sofa?

Relax Together is a private workshop where you can slow down, enjoy each other's company and discover simple ways to massage and relax each other. You'll take turns giving and receiving massage, explore gentle movement and breathing, and discover how touch, rocking and finding a shared rhythm can help you unwind together.

Inspired by Thai Yoga Massage and somatic dance, the techniques are easy to learn and adapt. You'll explore how to adjust pressure, notice each other's responses and communicate what feels comfortable, without unnecessary effort. It's about enjoying the experience, paying attention to each other and taking home a few practical ways to relax together in everyday life.

Whether you're romantic partners, friends or family members, the workshop is adapted to your needs and comfort.

No previous experience needed. All massage exercises are fully clothed, and you can choose what feels comfortable for you.

Practical details & booking

For an at-home workshop, you'll need enough clear floor space for two people to lie down and move comfortably — ideally around 8 m² (approximately 2.5 × 3.2 metres). You don't need a dedicated studio or an empty room. A living room with some furniture moved aside can work perfectly well. We'll discuss your space and any practical questions during the introductory video call.

Once we've agreed on a date and location, a €50 online booking payment confirms your workshop. The remaining amount is paid in cash on the day:

  • At home: €50 online + €50 cash on the day
  • In a studio: €50 online + €90 cash on the day.
    You'll receive the online payment details once we've agreed on the arrangements.

Plans can change! Please let me know as soon as possible if you need to change your arrangements.
The €50 online booking payment is non-refundable under the standard booking terms, but can be transferred to a new workshop date, subject to the conditions below:

At-home workshops
You can reschedule your workshop up to 3 days before agreed date and transfer your €50 booking payment to the new date. If you cancel instead, the booking payment is not refunded.

Studio workshops
Rescheduling or cancellation is possible only while the studio reservation can still be cancelled without charge: 10 days before the workshop day. I'll let you know the exact deadline before you confirm your booking. Once that deadline has passed, the workshop cannot be cancelled or rescheduled under the standard booking terms.
