## Proposed composition rules

Keep typography, hierarchy, photo treatment and logo placement consistent across
all outputs. Change only the featured workshop and necessary channel layout.
Between editions, vary one compatible accent palette and one shape variation.
The edition variant is explicit, not randomly selected for each image.

Event overviews show all workshop photos with balanced prominence. Workshop
Instagram posts show the featured photo prominently (roughly two-thirds of
photographic area as an initial layout hypothesis), with every other workshop
photo visible. Prepare layouts for 3–4, 5–6 and 7–8 photos.

only. Resolve it in the edition brief before production. Meetup text content is
